About Free Finder

Free Finder helps people discover useful free or free-tier alternatives to paid AI and software tools. We aim to explain limits clearly instead of calling trials or tiny credits “free”.

Privacy

This site does not require an account. Search queries are processed in your browser. When you follow an external tool link, that provider's own privacy policy applies.

Terms

Free Finder provides software-discovery information for general informational purposes. Prices, limits, availability and features can change. Verify important details on the provider's current official website.
